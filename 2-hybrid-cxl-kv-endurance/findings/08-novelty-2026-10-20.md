# Novelty search, 2026-10-20 (P2)

Still novel in combination (KV write rate and FTL WAF for a hybrid CXL DRAM+NAND device; LRU vs
request-scoped death; no TRIM on CXL.mem; real drive; mailbox discard), but the "no published study"
sentence must be narrowed: High-Bandwidth Flash (HBF) papers since 2026-08 estimate KV write rates,
GC WAF and lifetime.

Closest (must cite and position):
- HBFlex, arXiv:2609.18675: KV write rates per GPU; GC WAF ~30x (DeepSeek-V4-Pro), avg 11.98x, cut to
  1.24x/1.04x; says GC, not raw writes, dominates. Reconcile with our 2.1/5.2 and "write rate is the wall".
- Lachesis, arXiv:2610.08378: lifetime-aware HBM/HBF placement, assumes WAF 1.02 ("prior work"); our
  request-scoped 2.1/5.2 rebuts it.
- "HBF Sucks?", arXiv:2608.11668: Bailian traces, 48 to 140 TB/day KV writes exceed TLC/3-DWPD budgets;
  reuse-aware admission, write budgeting; no WAF.
- LRU-K admission for generative recommendation on HBF, arXiv:2609.07175 (admission prior).
- LM-CXD, arXiv:2609.26828: CXL-SSD with device-DRAM free queue for deleted chunks, erase-line-aligned
  chunks dropped at erase; no WAF/lifetime numbers. Contrast with mailbox discard + trim.
