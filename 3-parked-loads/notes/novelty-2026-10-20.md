# Novelty search, 2026-10-20 (P3)

Still novel: no work has a CXL Type-3 device hold a CXL.mem read of an unwritten line until another
host writes it, made safe against Root Port completion timeouts, credit exhaustion and host failure,
and model-checked. Lead the contribution with R1-R5 and the TLA+ check.
